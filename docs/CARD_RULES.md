## Engine behaviour

`src/lib/rules.ts` is versioned product knowledge. `engine.ts` is pure; `state.ts` owns personal records. Dates use **Asia/Singapore**, with calendar/statement intervals inclusive at their start and exclusive at their end. A statement day means the confirmed first day of the cycle, not an assumed billing date.

Opening bonus spend counts only when its period start matches. Unknown usage suppresses bonus estimates. Pending transactions reserve gross eligible spend before cap clipping; issuer rounding is applied when calculating capacity. They do not qualify welcome/minimum spend until posted, and late posting moves the full reservation into the correct period. Reversed records are excluded. Transactions retain the rule ID/version, source, estimate, eligibility, applied cap, offer contribution, warnings and explanation captured at recording. Later rule edits do not rewrite these snapshots. Timestamp inputs require an explicit timezone.

Ranking compares miles at the user's valuation plus cashback and newly achievable welcome/minimum-spend value, less FX and a user-confirmed per-mile transfer-cost allocation. FX calculations take an **SGD equivalent supplied by the user**, not a live exchange quote. A batch transfer fee is disclosed when no allocation is supplied; the engine does not invent a transfer size. Earlier minimum-spend estimates remain immutable even when a later purchase makes retroactive rewards achievable; the additional value appears in that later recommendation.

An MCC supplied without confirmed provenance remains **Unverified**. Missing MCCs can yield **Likely** conditional estimates using the selected category, never fabricated merchant codes. Confirmed exclusions override headline earn. Rules checked over six months ago carry a freshness warning. Unverified rules and manual-review benefits stay outside automated ranking.

Welcome value compares attainable tiers with and without the intended purchase plus explicit planned natural spending. Tiers already achievable without it receive no extra value. Unreachable tiers say **Not worth chasing**; pending spend and unconfirmed eligibility never unlock an offer.

Unsupported details remain visible boundaries: promotional cap upgrades, HSBC deposit boosts, refunds crossing issuer periods, merchant-specific exceptions, exact foreign settlement rates, and multi-month UOB qualification need additional verified knowledge/state before automated ranking. No banking credentials are part of this model.
