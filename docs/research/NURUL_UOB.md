Nurul confirmed standard Lady's; her current spending category, enrolment date, opening cap usage and KrisFlyer membership-year qualification remain unconfirmed. “Miles” identifies rewards, not a Lady's spending category. ## Calculation specification

### Lady's: selection and caps

| Variant | Selected categories | Monthly bonus limit | Base after cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Classic/Platinum/World | One | 1,800 bonus UNI$; S$1,000 equivalent | 0.4 mpd |
| Solitaire, since 1 August 2025 | Two | 1,350 bonus UNI$/S$750 **per category**; S$1,500 combined | 0.4 mpd |
| Solitaire Metal | Two | 3,600 bonus UNI$/S$2,000 combined | 0.4 mpd |

Within the selected category/cap, total earn is 4 mpd: 0.4 base plus 3.6 bonus. No monthly minimum spend applies. Missing selection means base only. Initial enrolment starts on enrolment date; later changes start next calendar quarter. The last choice submitted before 23:59 Singapore time before that quarter wins; an unchanged selection carries forward. Upgrading standard to Solitaire changes the existing category cap immediately, but the second category selection starts next quarter. [Official Lady's FAQ](https://www.uob.com.sg/assets/pdfs/faqs-final.pdf), questions 1–16.

Use the bank category, not our app's broad category alone: bars `5813` belong to Entertainment, grocery stores to Family, and fashion excludes ordinary electronics. Do not invent Travel eligibility for every travel agency or booking marketplace. [Lady's terms, Schedule 1](https://www.uob.com.sg/assets/pdfs/ladys-cards-tcs.pdf), August 2025 version 1.1.

Posting date assigns calendar-month usage, not statement month or purchase date. Base points appear monthly; bonus points arrive by the following month's end. Foreign purchases use the bank's posted SGD amount. UOB$ transactions now earn Lady's base points as well as selected-category bonus, effective 1 November 2024. [Lady's terms](https://www.uob.com.sg/assets/pdfs/ladys-cards-tcs.pdf), clauses 8, 10, 33.

**Rounding:** base is per transaction; the 9X bonus aggregates monthly eligible spending. The official example uses S$518 + S$182 + S$100: base `103+36+20=159 UNI$`, bonus `800/5×9=1,440 UNI$`. [Lady's Savings FAQ](https://www.uob.com.sg/assets/pdfs/lsa-faq.pdf), question 11. Recommended conservative calculation: base `2×floor(purchase/5)` miles; bonus delta `18×[floor(min(monthSpend+purchase,cap)/5)-floor(min(monthSpend,cap)/5)]`. The example does not resolve fractional final S$5 blocks; flag that rounding assumption until checked against a statement. Opening eligible spend must be included in the monthly aggregate. A single transaction-rounding rule for both components undercounts bonus.

Lady's all-reward excluded MCCs: `4829,4900,5199,5960,5965,5993,6012,6050,6051,6211,6300,6513,6529,6530,6534,6540,7349,7511,7523,7995,8062,8211,8220,8241,8244,8249,8299,8398,8651,8661,8699,8999,9211,9222,9223,9311,9399,9402,9405`. Description exclusions include `AMAZE*`, `PAYPAL*`, `NORWDS*`, AXS, prepaid/EZ-Link/FlashPay/YouTrip, IPAYMY and specified brokerage/payment descriptions. LuxePay, instalments, NETS, fees, cash advances, funding/transfers and reversed transactions do not earn ordinary points. Preserve the issuer's complete descriptor list rather than treating all intermediaries as direct merchant purchases. [Lady's terms](https://www.uob.com.sg/assets/pdfs/ladys-cards-tcs.pdf), clauses 32–35.

### KrisFlyer: confirmed partner and annual condition

The S$1,000 annual airline-group condition applies to membership years ending November 2025 onward. The previous S$800 condition applied only through October 2025. The accelerator dropped from 1.8 to 1.2 additional mpd for transactions posted from 1 June 2025; it is not currently 3 mpd. [Current UOB card brochure](https://www.uob.com.sg/web-resources/personal/pdf/personal/useful/personal-resource/credit-cards/card-combined-form-online.pdf), KrisFlyer section; [product announcement](https://www.uob.com.sg/personal/cards/travel/krisflyer-card.page).

Membership year starts with the **approval month**, spanning twelve complete calendar months (May approval → May through April; model end as next 1 May exclusive). Base/partner miles are calculated monthly and credited directly to the principal's own linked KrisFlyer account. Deferred miles arrive in the month after membership-year end, not upon crossing the threshold. Supplementary spending pools into that principal account. All ordinary earn uses complete S$5 blocks per transaction; no ordinary earn cap is stated. [KrisFlyer terms](https://www.uob.com.sg/assets/pdfs/kf_credit_card_full_tnc.pdf), clauses 1.5–1.8, 1.15 and definitions.

**Engine decisions:** use a user-confirmed partner enum rather than substring inference. Distinguish bank bonus miles from merchant/app bonus miles. Keep base-only ranking until the annual period and qualifying spend are complete/known and the condition is achieved; show deferred eligibility separately without encouraging airline spending. Never mutate past reward snapshots when a later purchase completes qualification. Pending posting dates and uncertain MCC/online classification remain visible assumptions. Selected categories should carry an effective date: reconfirming the same choice should not discard the true initial enrolment date.

Nurul has multiple UOB cards, so the single-card automatic waiver statement does not establish her eligibility.
