Scope: Singapore **DBS Woman's World Mastercard**, not the ordinary Woman's Card, and **Maybank XL Rewards**, not XL Cashback. No signup gift or new-card condition is assumed.

## DBS Woman's World: calculation specification

Current nominal rates are 4 mpd on eligible online spending, 1.2 mpd on eligible foreign-currency retail and 0.4 mpd on other eligible retail. There is no ordinary minimum-spend requirement. The first **S$1,000 qualifying online spend per calendar month** shares one cap across local and foreign online transactions and principal/supplementary cards. Above the cap, local spending returns to 0.4 mpd and foreign spending to 1.2 mpd. Foreign and online rates do not stack beyond 4 mpd. [Product](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/cards/credit-cards/dbs-woman-mastercard-card), [operative terms](https://www.dbs.com.sg/iwov-resources/media/pdf/cards/dbs-womans-card-tnc.pdf), updated 27 July 2026, clauses 17–19.

Exact award components, measured in DBS Points; one point converts to two KrisFlyer/Asia Miles:

| Component | Formula | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retail base | `floor(transaction SGD / 5 × 1)` | Next working day after posting |
| Additional foreign base | `floor(transaction SGD / 5 × 2)` | Next working day after posting |
| Local online bonus | `floor(monthly qualifying local online SGD / 5 × 9)` | End of following calendar month |
| Foreign online bonus | `floor(monthly qualifying foreign online SGD / 5 × 7)` | End of following calendar month |

The two online bonus aggregates are separate but consume the same **gross S$1,000 cap**. This is point-component flooring, not rounding the whole transaction down to a S$5 spend block. With no prior spend, a local S$512 online purchase earns 2,046 miles; foreign S$512 earns 2,044. Two local S$2.50 online purchases earn no base but together 18 bonus miles. Exact incremental bonus needs prior local/foreign accumulated eligible amounts; a single opening cap balance cannot reconstruct those remainders. Do not invent them. [Terms](https://www.dbs.com.sg/iwov-resources/media/pdf/cards/dbs-womans-card-tnc.pdf), clauses 17b, 18b, 19b–d.

Calendar attribution uses the bank's **transaction date**, defined as merchant/acquirer settlement date, subject to timezone, and requires successful posting by the bonus computation. It is not necessarily the receipt date or posting-month. Online status depends on issuer/acquirer card-not-present/Internet indicators, not merely an app label. MOTO, telephone and online banking payments do not become eligible online purchases; instalment postings and payment/funding channels are excluded. [Terms](https://www.dbs.com.sg/iwov-resources/media/pdf/cards/dbs-womans-card-tnc.pdf), clauses 11–14, 19c.

### Exclusions: preserve both layers

General rewards exclusions remove all ordinary DBS Points. They include fees, cash advances, instalments, bills/AXS/SAM, funding/prepaid/cryptocurrency, insurance, utilities, education, government, financial services, specified hospitals, parking/tolls, charities and specified merchant descriptions such as AMAZE, YouTrip and wallet top-ups. The online programme has a **larger** MCC/descriptor exclusion schedule: additional categories can lose online bonus while retaining an otherwise eligible base rate. Do not collapse these layers or blanket-exclude every PayPal purchase. ## Maybank XL Rewards: calculation specification

Eligible local Dine/Shop/Travel/Play MCCs and foreign-currency retail earn nominal **4 mpd** when at least **S$500 eligible retail** is charged in the calendar month; otherwise 0.4 mpd. Other eligible retail is 0.4 mpd. Online status alone does not qualify local purchases. Principal and supplementary spending combine. [XL product](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/cards/credit/maybank-xl-card.page), [XL terms](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/iwov-resources/sg/pdf/cards/xl-privileges-tp-tnc.pdf), effective 1 December 2025.

| Local category | MCCs |
| --- | --- |
| Dine | `5462,5811,5812,5814` |
| Shop | `5262,5310,5311,5331,5399,5621,5631,5651,5655,5661,5691,5699,5941` |
| Travel | `3000–3299,3300–3308,4511,4722,7011` |
| Play | `4899,5813,5815,7832,7993,7994` |

Standard cap is **10,000 TREATS awarded at the total 10X rate per calendar month**. 1X is five TREATS/S$5 and 25,000 TREATS convert to 10,000 airline miles, so 10X is fifty TREATS/S$5, equal to 4 mpd. The derived capacity is **S$1,000 rounded qualifying spend**; above it, base continues. Do not add 10X to base and show 4.4 mpd. Clause 4.1's poorly labelled examples S$129→125 points and S$250→250 points describe **base 1X**, consistent with general rewards terms and the product's explicit 4 mpd. Exact cap denomination is points, not an explicitly stated S$1,000 spend ceiling. [XL terms](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/iwov-resources/sg/pdf/cards/xl-privileges-tp-tnc.pdf), clauses 1.1, 3.3–3.4, 4.1; [general terms](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/iwov-resources/sg/pdf/cards/terms_and_conditions.pdf), clause 2.1; [conversion catalogue](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/cards/rewards/catalogue.page).

Spend rounds down **per purchase to complete S$5 blocks** for points. Points arrive by end of the following calendar month or a bank-selected date. For **minimum-spend** attribution, purchases charged in one month and posted by the tenth of the following month count in the original month; those posted later count toward the following month's minimum. Applying the same grace period to cap attribution is an implementation inference, not an explicit cap clause. Refunds/reversals remove rewards. The XL terms mention GST excluded from points but do not provide transaction-tax identification; do not infer an arbitrary GST deduction from gross merchant charges. [XL terms](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/iwov-resources/sg/pdf/cards/xl-privileges-tp-tnc.pdf), clauses 3.5, 4.1–4.2.

### Fees, expiry and transfers

Principal annual fee **S$87.20 incl. GST**, first two years waived; subsequent automatic waiver requires **S$6,000 annual spend**. Supplementary cards are free. Foreign-currency fees are 2.25% bank plus network up to 1%, so **up to 3.25%**; SGD processed overseas/DCC up to 1% instead, merchant FX spread additional. Avoid assuming DCC earns foreign rewards. [Product](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/cards/credit/maybank-xl-card.page), [fee schedule](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/bank-charges/cc/credit-cards.page).

Airline conversion: 25,000 TREATS→10,000 KrisFlyer; 12,500→5,000 Asia Miles or Enrich; 4,000→2,000 AirAsia points. Fee **S$27.25 incl. GST per conversion**; same cardmember's own airline account, not another person's. Completion seven–fourteen working days, KrisFlyer up to fifteen. Vouchers/cash credits have different catalogue values. [Catalogue](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/cards/rewards/catalogue.page), [general terms](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/iwov-resources/sg/pdf/cards/terms_and_conditions.pdf).

Ordinary TREATS expire one year from their earning quarter; statement/app expiry controls. Rewards Infinite membership can remove expiry while active but requires **S$24,000 eligible spend over preceding twelve months** across named cards including XL; membership/birthday discounted redemption is conditional. On RI termination/non-renewal, rolled points have special forfeiture/expiry provisions; the current general document's clauses 9.5/9.7 are not fully consistent. Do not set non-expiry merely because XL is owned or promise rollover. [General terms](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/iwov-resources/sg/pdf/cards/terms_and_conditions.pdf), [RI catalogue](https://www.maybank2u.com.sg/en/personal/cards/rewards/catalogue.page).
