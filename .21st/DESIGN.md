# Our Miles design context

Selected direction: **Field Notes**. A personal, editorial travel instrument for Aleem and Nurul, centered on a fast card decision. Aleem explicitly reselected this original design on 7 October 2026. Restore its ivory, serif and forest-green identity while retaining the merchant lookup, recommendation improvements and authentic card artwork.

Family resemblance: local Instrument Serif and ivory from `our_flight` / `our_gallery`. Forest green marks actions and the winner. Use typography, whitespace and sparse dividers before containers. Four primary destinations: Home, What Card?, Wallet, Activity. Goals and Bonuses are secondary, reachable from relevant content.

Framework: Next.js, React, strict TypeScript, Tailwind v4. Radix Dialog provides sheet behavior. Motion uses reduced-motion-aware lightweight state transitions. Original route mark and vector landscape; issuer card artwork is served locally with source records in [docs/CARD_ARTWORK.md](../docs/CARD_ARTWORK.md).

Canonical tokens and interaction patterns: [docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](../docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Exploration rationale: [docs/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md](../docs/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md). Actual rendered quality gate: [docs/DESIGN_REVIEW.md](../docs/DESIGN_REVIEW.md).

Preserve ownership, explicit uncertainty, current cap quantities, immutable recorded explanations and demo isolation. Never rank by headline earn alone or introduce decorative dashboard conventions.

Merchant search, textual MCC confidence, direct card setup and conditional reward comparisons remain functional additions within Field Notes. Card images support recognition; card name and owner remain readable text. Keep fixed sheet headers and comfortable controls when adding these features to the established layouts.
